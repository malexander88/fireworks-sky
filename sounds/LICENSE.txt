Fireworks Sky – sound credits
=============================

All sound effects are synthesized in the browser with the Web Audio API:

  - launch whoosh (filtered noise sweep + rising tone)
  - burst boom (soft low sine + low-passed noise)
  - burst pop (shorter, higher thud)
  - glitter crackle (a handful of quiet high-pass clicks)
  - sparkler fizz (soft high-pass noise while swiping)
  - moon giggle (short rising sine / triangle notes)

Background music is also generated in the game: a gentle C-major arpeggio
and pad at 100 BPM. Firework bursts are loosely nudged onto the beat when
they would land close to one.

No third-party recordings are used, so no attribution is required.
The same note appears in the game under the “ⓘ sounds” button.

Volumes are kept deliberately soft and child-friendly.
